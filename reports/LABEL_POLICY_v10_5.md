# Outcome label policy v10.5

## Class 0 — Failed / no useful coordination product

Use only when the experimental record explicitly reports no isolable product, no reaction, decomposition, or a product shown not to be the intended coordination material.

## Class 1 — Amorphous, uncertain or insufficiently verified

Use for precipitates without adequate structural confirmation, amorphous products, mixtures, poorly crystalline materials, conflicting characterization, or outcomes that cannot be assigned reliably to classes 0 or 2. This is an uncertainty class, not a weaker success class.

## Class 2 — Crystalline MOF

Use only when diffraction evidence supports a crystalline coordination framework consistent with the claimed product. The preferred evidence is PXRD matched to a reference/simulated pattern or a solved single-crystal structure.

## Required provenance for newly curated records

Each new record should contain a DOI or stable source identifier, explicit evidence type, extraction method, reviewer status, and a ligand SMILES/InChIKey. Conflicting outcomes under an identical normalized condition signature must be reviewed rather than silently deduplicated.

## Legacy-data limitation

The v8 development data do not contain source DOI or row-level PXRD verification. Their historical labels are preserved, but class 2 cannot retrospectively be described as PXRD-verified until source-level recuration is complete.
