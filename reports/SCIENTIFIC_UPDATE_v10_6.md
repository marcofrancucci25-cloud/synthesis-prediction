# Scientific update v10.6.0

Twenty unique laboratory experiments were extracted from the DDS workbook and normalized to one experiment per row. Room temperature was standardized to 25 °C; minute-based procedures were converted to hours; missing DDS8/DDS9 temperatures were recovered from the written procedure; mixed-solvent volumes, precursor hydration, ligand identities and outcome evidence were made explicit.

Seventeen records passed the principal evidence gate: three failed syntheses, one amorphous/uncertain product and thirteen PXRD-supported crystalline outcomes. DDS1 remains in review because the record states both that no precipitate formed and that an amorphous XRD pattern was obtained. DDS10,2 and DDS10,3 remain in a separate in-situ ibuprofen dataset.

The integrated evidence database contains 1,095 records. The positive library was audited globally: 48 repeated condition-signature entries in v10.4 were consolidated while preserving their source identifiers. Ten unique laboratory condition templates were added, producing a v10.6 positive library with 656 unique condition signatures.

The v8 predictor was not retrained. Its numerical ranges, categorical applicability and reported external metrics remain tied to the original frozen 1,078-row training database. Laboratory evidence affects similar-record retrieval and positive-template optimization only.
